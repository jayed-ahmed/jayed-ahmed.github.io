Measurement of Human Serum TSH Using Sandwich ELISA

  • Objective: Quantitative determination of Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH) levels in human serum samples to assess thyroid function[cite: 1].
  • Work Done: Performed sample dispensing, standard dilution, antibody-enzyme conjugate incubation, washing steps, and optical density reading.
  • Methods & Tools: 96-well monoclonal anti-TSH microplate, HRP-conjugated antibody, TMB substrate, spectrophotometry (450 nm).
  • Result: Generated a standard calibration curve to accurately evaluate serum TSH concentrations.
